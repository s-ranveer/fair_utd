01 / The institute

FAIR brings fundamental questions about intelligence into conversation with the systems we can build.

Rooted in the breadth of UT Dallas, the institute connects researchers across computer science, engineering, cognitive science, and related fields. Together, we study the foundations of AI and translate insight into responsible progress.

02 / Research
